Executive takeaway (open the meeting with this)

  1. We are losing ~28% of GMV between order and delivery - EGP 250M over 8 months.
  2. The biggest lever is post-closure logistics, not seller performance. Sellers reach module closure on 80% of GMV (Seller GTN 80.08%) but only 71.56% reaches the customer. The 8.5pp gap = EGP ~75M lost AFTER the seller does their job. OCF gap is 17.5pp; MDS 12.8pp.
  3. OCF sellers are the strongest performers (Seller GTN 86.6%) but OCF logistics is the leak. Fixing OCF last-mile is the highest-ROI single move.
  4. ~60 sellers are overloaded; ~17 strong sellers are starved of volume. Rebalance is no-cost.
  5. Tier sellers on Seller GTN going forward (not Overall GTN) - they shouldn't be punished for logistics issues.
  6. Estimated combined uplift: +6-8 pp Overall GTN within 2 quarters (~EGP 70M/year recovered run-rate).

SKU SLA Right-Sizing (Assigned vs Actual)

Takeaway: Assigned SLA = Seller Expected Delivery − Confirmation. Actual = Seller Closure − Confirmation. The gap tells you whether the SKU's promised lead time is realistic.
How to read: P90 actual = the lead time 90% of the seller's items hit. If P90 is much HIGHER than Assigned SLA → SKU is over-promised; sellers can't hit it → increase SLA. If P90 is much LOWER than Assigned SLA → SKU is under-promised; we're wasting customer perception → decrease SLA. Filter: closed items only, ≥30 items per SKU+Seller, test sellers excluded.

SKUs that need to INCREASE assigned SLA (top 25)

P90 actual lead time exceeds promised SLA by ≥2 days — sellers cannot hit the current commitment. Risk: cancellations & customer dissatisfaction.

SKUs that can DECREASE assigned SLA (top 25)

Assigned SLA is ≥3 days higher than P90 actual — current promise is too loose. Opportunity: sharper customer experience without operational cost.

C-Level Discussion Guide

15-minute meeting structure

Suggested Meeting Flow (15 min)

  1. Opening (1 min): "We are losing ~28% of GMV between order and delivery - EGP 250M in 8 months. Plan recovers ~5-6 pp GTN within 2 quarters."
  2. Headline KPIs (2 min): Six KPI tiles. Anchor on GTN 71.56% and EGP 250M loss.
  3. Root causes (3 min): Three drivers: cancellations, rejections, returns. Highlight dominant reason in each.
  4. Where the leak happens (3 min): Funnel - drop-off after Confirmation = vendor-side. Module table - FDS lags SLA, OCF leads.
  5. Capacity story (2 min): Overloaded vs underutilized. Volume rebalance is no-cost.
  6. Action plan (3 min): Six quick wins for Q1; commit owners and timelines.
  7. Asks (1 min): Approval for SLA penalty structure (M2), tech bandwidth for COD risk filter (S6), Commercial buy-in for vendor tiering (M1).

Likely C-Level Questions & Suggested Answers

Q: Why is GTN flat MoM if we have so many initiatives?
A: Most prior initiatives addressed symptoms (post-cancel comms) not root causes (seller lead time + reassignment governance).
Q: Which single move has the biggest payback?
A: Reducing cancellations 30% via S1 + S6 - addressable EGP ~37M GMV recovered annually.
Q: What's the cheapest action?
A: M6 - rebalance volume from overloaded to underutilized sellers. Zero capex.
Q: What's the riskiest assumption?
A: That sellers will improve under SLA penalties. Mitigation: graduated escalation (M2).
Q: How do we measure success?
A: Single source-of-truth GTN dashboard (L6) refreshed daily; monthly steerco.
Q: What if we do nothing?
A: Status quo loses ~EGP 31M/month in non-recoverable GMV.

Data Quality & Methodology Notes

  • All metrics computed strictly on the 128,496 order items provided.
  • SLA evaluable on items with both Seller Closure Date and Seller Expected Delivery Date populated.
  • Module-specific closure: MDS = min(DC, Shipped) | OCF = RTS | FDS = Delivered.
  • Logistics LT computed only for MDS/OCF (FDS has no separate handover-to-delivered leg).
  • Reassignment: SKUs with >=2 sellers and >=20 items combined; "Latest" = most recent Last Order date.
  • Capacity: Overloaded = monthly volume >=P75 AND SLA <median. Underutilized = monthly volume <=P25 AND SLA >=median AND GTN >=median.
  • Uplift estimates are order-of-magnitude based on action effectiveness assumptions.
